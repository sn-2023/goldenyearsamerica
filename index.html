How it works

You arrange everything from the US, on your schedule. We take care of what happens at your parents' home.

  1. Tell us about your parents

    Fill in the short form below or message us on WhatsApp. We'll call you at a time that works in your US time zone.

  2. We meet them at home

    The Mallis Home Care team visits your parents, understands what they need, and suggests the right kind of help.

  3. Care begins, and you stay close

    A caregiver starts at your parents' home, and you get updates on WhatsApp so you always know how they're doing.

Help for every part of their day

Choose one service or combine a few. Care can be daytime, full-time or live-in, depending on what your parents need.

Patient care
Care for parents who are unwell, bedridden or recovering: bathing, feeding, hygiene and comfort, always with dignity.
Home nurse
Registered nurses at home for injections, dressings, checking vitals, medicines and recovery after a hospital stay.
Baby care
Trained help for newborns and new mothers, whether the birth is in India or family is visiting.
Cooking
Fresh, home-style meals made in their own kitchen, suited to their diet and taste.
Attender
A dependable companion for elderly parents: daily help, walks, hospital visits and medicines on time.
Housekeeping
Home maids for cleaning and daily chores, so your parents' home stays comfortable and safe.
